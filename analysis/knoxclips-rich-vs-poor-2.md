# Breakdown: "The Difference Between Rich And Poor #2" (@KnoxClips7000)

Source: a phone screen recording (888×1920, ~15 s), analysed frame by frame at 30 fps.
The recording covers only ~13 s of the Short: it starts mid-sentence and stops
before the payoff. Audio could not be machine-transcribed here, so the script below
is read from the burned-in captions.

## Script (as captioned, partial)

> [He takes] away everyone's wealth. Every coin, every house, every field: gone.
> Everyone is poor. Everyone is hungry. 3 days later, he gives each person one
> chicken. The first group is so hungry, they cook their chicken that same night.

How it's written:
- **Parable / thought experiment.** A god-like hand resets the world, then hands everyone the same resource. "The first group…" sets up a comparison with a second group (the part cut off), which is where the "rich vs poor" lesson lands.
- **Rule of three:** "Every coin, every house, every field: gone." The colon and one-word punch ("gone.") end the list.
- **Parallel repetition:** "Everyone is poor. Everyone is hungry."
- **Very short declarative sentences**, present tense, no filler, no "imagine if".
- **Time jumps as beats:** "3 days later," gets its own shot.
- **Pace:** ~38 words in ~13 s ≈ 2.9 words/s (~175 wpm).

## Look: style, lighting, contrast, shadow

- **Style:** 3D environments with toon shading, and simple characters with 2D-style faces and black outlines. Low-detail, clean, toy-like; readable at phone size.
- **Lighting:** one warm directional "sun" key with a bright sky fill. Soft-edged cast shadows on the ground; volumetric god rays behind the hand for the divine moments. At night the **campfire becomes the key light** (warm orange from below) against a blue-purple ambient.
- **Shadows as storytelling:** "3 days later" is shown with long shadows stretching across the plaza, not with a title card alone.

Contrast stays moderate. Day shots are high-key with no crushed blacks (shadow floor ~50–130), and night drops the overall level but keeps faces readable.
